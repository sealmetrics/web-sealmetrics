### 6.1 Downloadable sector studies

On some pages we publish studies of what AI models say about a sector, such as [the one on Mallorca hotels](/es/ai-brand-monitoring/hoteles-mallorca/) (in Spanish). The full report of each study is requested with your email address, and we send it to you by email.

- **Data:** your email address and the study you ask for. We do not ask for your name. We note whether the address belongs to a free email provider, to tell apart the requests that come from companies.
- **Purposes and bases:**
  - (a) sending you the study, because you asked for it (Art. 6(1)(b));
  - (b) keeping a record of the request to prevent abuse and handle enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f));
  - (c) occasionally sending you studies and news, **only if you tick the optional box** (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 21 LSSI). Not ticking it does not affect the study, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.
- **Recipients:**
  - **Cloudflare, Inc.:** performs the bot check (Turnstile) and receives the form.
  - **Resend, Inc. (USA):** delivers the study and, if you consented, the occasional emails. It fetches the PDF from sealmetrics.com; the PDF contains no data about you.
  - Neither Enroutia nor the AI models receive anything: the study is done before you ask for it.
- **Retention:**
  - Request record: 24 months.
  - Occasional email list: until you unsubscribe.
